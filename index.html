If I Were Elena, You'd Be My Damon 🖤
My Nikk, You know what's funny? You can spend your whole life thinking you understand love, thinking you know what you want, thinking your heart is perfectly capable of protecting itself. And then one person walks into your life, looks at you a certain way, and suddenly all your little theories about love become completely useless. That's what you did to me, baby. I still remember that railway station. I remember seeing you for the first time, especially your eyes. Sometimes I wish I could go back to that exact moment, just to experience it again—the uncertainty, the butterflies, that first look before I knew how much you would come to mean to me. If I could meet you for the first time all over again, knowing how hopelessly crazy I'd become about you, I'd still want to see those eyes. And honestly? You're ridiculously handsome. It's actually unfair. Who gave you permission to look that good and then be sweet to me, too? Pick a struggle, Nikk. 😘🖤 But it's not just your face, your charm, or that confidence of yours. It's the person underneath all of it. The vulnerable parts you don't always show. The loyalty, the tenderness, the little things that make you you. I love the way you make me laugh, the way you make me feel, and the way you've become such a real part of my world. Here's my confession, baby: I'm not just in love with you. I've fallen for you in a way I can't neatly explain, take back, or pretend doesn't exist. You matter to me more than my pride knows how to admit, and when I imagine the person I want beside me through all the beautiful, difficult, ordinary days of life, it's you I keep coming back to. And maybe that's the scariest thing about loving someone. You can't control it like a switch. You can't always explain why one person's voice can change your entire mood, why one look can stay in your mind for hours, or why the thought of them can make an ordinary day feel different. I just know that you're the one who has made me feel the most alive. I love your confidence, your sass, your soft side, and even that little bit of trouble you carry around with you. You can be charming, infuriating, sweet, and completely impossible—and somehow, my heart still looks at you and says, Yep. That's my person. 😂❤️ I don't need our story to look like a television show. I don't need us to be perfect, and I don't expect every day to feel like a dramatic confession in the rain. I want the real thing. The laughter, the silly fights, the making up, the long conversations, the quiet moments, and the future we get to build one day at a time. So, on your birthday, I wish I could let you borrow my eyes for just a minute. Maybe then you'd understand why you're so special to me. Maybe you'd understand why that first look at the railway station is still a memory I want to replay. Happy birthday, my baby. My Nikk. My boyfiee. My husband. ❤️ If I could return to the beginning of our story, I'd still want to know you. I'd still want to hear your voice. I'd still want that first look. And if I had to choose all over again, with my eyes wide open and my heart knowing exactly what it was getting into, I'd still choose you. No supernatural powers required. Although, between us, you already have a pretty ridiculous effect on me. 😏🖤 All my love, Your Divya ❤️
